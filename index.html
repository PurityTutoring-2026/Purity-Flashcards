How to use these flashcards
  1. Pick a deck from the Deck menu, or keep All decks to mix every topic.
  2. Read the question on the card and say your answer out loud before you look.
  3. Flip the card by tapping it, pressing Show answer, or pressing the Space bar.
  4. Rate yourself and move on: press Got it › if you knew it, or Review again › if you did not. Either button also records your answer and moves you to the next card. Read the Pearl on the back for the exam tip first.
  5. Press Next › at any time to go to the next card. Press ‹ Previous card to go back one card.
  6. At the end of a round, choose Study the ones I missed to repeat only your weak cards.
  7. Tick Only cards I have not mastered to skip cards you already know.
  8. Use Browse all to see every card as a list and search by topic or keyword (for example, lithium or Beers).

On a phone, flip the card, then swipe right for Got it or left for Review again to go to the next card. On a computer: Space flips, → next card, ← previous card, 2 = Got it, 1 = Review again. Your progress is saved in this browser on this device only; clearing your browser data or using a different device starts fresh.
